## Goal
This room is teaching me about being a junior security analyst.


## Responsibilities
- Monitor and investigate various security alerts
- Participate in SOC brainstorms and workshops
- Cooperate with other teams to keep your company safe
- Constantly learn and discover new attacks and defenses


## Roles
- Senior analyst helps and resolves issues for the junior analyst and handle the complex cases.
- SOC Engineer doesn't have shift or analize security alerts, they maintain the security tools and configure the alerts to make analyst jobs easier.
- SOC Manager reports all SOC results to top management.
- Incident Responder is called on demand for major incidents.
