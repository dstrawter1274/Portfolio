## Goal

## Key Ideas
- **CPU:** (Central Processing Unit) aka Processor, it's the brain of the computer, Carries out instructions and processes data.
- **RAM:** (Random Access Memory) is the short term or working memory of the computer, it holds the data that the CPU needs quick access to. RAM is volatile!
- **Storage:** HHD/SSD is the long term memory of the computer, permanently keeps saved data until deleted even when powered off.
- **Motherboard:** Connects the computer’s components so they can communicate and holds them in place, It's the skeleton and nerves of the computer.
- **Network Adapter** is the vocal cords of the computer, allows to communicate with networks and other computers.
- **Power Supply Unit** PSU is essential and is basically the heart of the computer, it distributes electricity to everything inside of the computer and needs the appropiate output.
- **Graphics Card** is the visual cortex of the computer, it displays all received information from programs and operating system.
- **Input/Output** These devices puts information in the computer or gets information from the computer.
